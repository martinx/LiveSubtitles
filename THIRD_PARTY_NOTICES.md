## Speech models (not redistributed)

Model weights are **not** included in this repository or in any release binary. They are
downloaded from Hugging Face on first use, into
`~/Library/Application Support/FluidAudio/Models/`, and remain subject to their own
licences — including:

- **Parakeet EOU / Parakeet Unified / Nemotron** (NVIDIA), as converted and published by
  Fluid Inference. See the FluidAudio
  [model catalog](https://github.com/FluidInference/FluidAudio/blob/main/Documentation/Models.md)
  for the per-model licence and source.

If you redistribute a build that bundles model weights, you are responsible for carrying
their licences too.
